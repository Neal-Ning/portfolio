# Junction X Delft Hackathon 2nd place winner

### The hackathon

Junction X Delft is a 24 hour hackathon for teams of 5 located in Delft University, Netherlands. Our topic is to design a system that screens freely available speech data for hate speech to enable development of inclusive speech technology. 

---

### Our solution

Hate speech can manifest in subtle ways through implications, semantics and context. Instead of fully relying on rigid classification models or universal definitions of hate speech, we built a consensus-driven AI framework, where we model human with diverse perspectives and customizable backgrounds using simulated AI society agents. Through persuation and voting, the agents collectively determine whether a piece of presented content is extremist or not. 

This approach reduces bias by capturing nuanced social interpretations of language. It also provides sociologists and developers with a configurable tool to define hate speech boundaries, tailor inclusivity goals, and gain educational insight through transparent AI debate processes.
